2.  Missing female voices.  For those interested in providing a female voice, here is the basic words, their translation, and how to pronounce them.
(Note, whenever a [u] is shown spoken as a [w], it is merely there for vocal transition, meaning you speak it as little as possible.  Same goes for [tl] in the original script, though I just simply removed the [l] in the pronunciation guide.)

"I will guard" - ni tlamokuitlauis - ni ta-mo-ki-tawis
